Frequently Asked Questions

What currency is used on Taniti?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar as its currency. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several banks facilitate currency exchange and many businesses accept major credit cards.

What are the alcohol laws?

Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m. The drinking age on Taniti is 18 and is not strictly enforced.

What are the power outlets like?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Is it safe to visit?

Violent crime is very rare on Taniti. However, as tourism increases, there are more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes. Visitors should take normal precautions with their belongings.

Is English spoken on the island?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Is there medical care available?

There is one hospital and several clinics on the island. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Are there national holidays I should be aware of?

Taniti enjoys a large number of national holidays. Many tourist attractions and restaurants will be closed on holidays, so visitors should plan accordingly.
