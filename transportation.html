Getting to and Around Taniti

Arriving on Taniti

By Air

Taniti is served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. The airport is currently being expanded so larger jets will be able to land within the next few years. Most visitors arrive by air.

By Cruise Ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay once per week for one night.

Getting Around

Public Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

Private Buses

Private buses serve the rest of the island outside of Taniti City.

Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Rental Cars

Rental cars can be rented from a local agency near the airport.

Bikes

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. The area surrounding Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.
