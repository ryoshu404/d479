Things to Do

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and visit the volcano. There is something for every type of traveler.

Outdoor

  • Beaches
  • Rainforest hikes
  • Volcano visits
  • Snorkeling
  • Zip-lining in the rainforest
  • Chartered fishing tours
  • Helicopter rides
  • Boat and bus tours of the island

Cultural

  • Local history museum
  • Art galleries

Entertainment

  • Dance club
  • Microbrewery and pubs
  • Movie theater
  • Arcade
  • Bowling
  • Nine-hole golf course (opening soon)

Many activities are located in Merriton Landing on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. Most tourists also spend time in Taniti City, which features native architecture and nearby white, sandy beaches.
